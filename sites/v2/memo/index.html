How I’d sell it: PROOF

  1. PPick the query that hurts, and the bar. 50 queries their agent sent last week; pass/fail signed first.
  2. RRun it in their harness. The kit drops into OpenAI, Anthropic or MCP tool calls; adding the as-of month lifted fresh useful results 2.1 to 4.1.
  3. OOpen the evidence. A one-page verdict memo, caveats included; labels checked against a second model (Claude), 28/30, no human validation yet.
  4. OOrder: size the deal. Measured volume × list price; Head of Revenue owns final terms.
  5. FFan out, and feed back. Wins become platform templates (Fireworks next); misses reach product.
