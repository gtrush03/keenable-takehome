Objections → answers · 1 of 2

Six objections I expect, answered.

"We already use Exa or Tavily."
Their date filters fence on publish date; on Keenable's engine that still leaked 29/70.
"Is query_time today's copy of an old URL?"
0/60 acquired after the cutoff, but 1/60 served later text. Proposed fix: versioned snapshots.
"You rank aggregators first."
True on finance: primary source in top 10 on 14/40. site: plus fetch today.

Objections → answers · 2 of 2

Crawl, cost and latency, answered.

"Common Crawl is free."
75.4% of 500 sampled pages have no URL match in a 2026 crawl. The gap is coverage.
"We already crawl."
Existing-team low scenario: ~$0.86M a year. Sell Delta, priced per novel document.
"Latency at our RPS?"
300/300 OK to 2 rps keyless. 100 RPS gets a contracted load test in the pilot.

Full lists: opt2_thesis.md §6 · opt1_thesis.md §8

Caveats · 1 of 2

What these numbers do not prove.

n = 7 events
The fence result covers seven market events. A pilot runs 200 of the buyer's.
Keyless endpoint
Every call went through /v1/search/public. There, pro and realtime returned identical lists.
Residential latency
Client-side from a San Francisco Mac, last mile included. Not a server SLO.

Caveats · 2 of 2

Still unproven: rivals at scale, forecasts, labels.

One rival run
Seven rivals ran once on 1 Oct, free tiers, 7 events and up to 40 queries, snippets only. Small n; see the head-to-head.
Monte Carlo
A bounds argument over ranged inputs, not a forecast.
Jev labels
Checked against a second model (Claude) on 30 fence results: 28/30. No human validation yet.
