How the test works

7 market events with a cutoff the day before the news broke (SVB, Credit Suisse, FTX, Activision, Nvidia, SBF sentencing, Keenable × Baseten). One query per event, top 10 results, each provider with its own best native date filter.

Keenable query_time fences on when the page was acquired. Every other filter fences on the page’s published date, which pages misreport.

How it is judged

“After cutoff” is a date check: acquired_at (Keenable) or published_at ≥ cutoff. Undated results never count as after.
